## Vietnam promotion law — what actually applies to SUNOI
Per Nghị định 81/2018/NĐ-CP and its 2026 amendment (Nghị định 239/2026/NĐ-CP):
- **Max discount**: 50% of pre-promotion price (up to 100% only in officially concentrated sales events like Black Friday)
- **Sở Công Thương notification** (normally required ≥3 working days before a promotion) is **exempted** when either: (a) total prize/gift value is under 100 million VND, or (b) the promotion runs only through an e-commerce platform (GrabFood/ShopeeFood) — **both exemptions apply to SUNOI at this scale**, so platform-run vouchers don't need advance notification
- **Prohibited**: running promotions physically at schools, hospitals, or government offices
- **Practical risk**: small, informally-operating home sellers are rarely inspected unless there's a major consumer complaint or very large scale — but stay within the 50% cap regardless, to avoid any unfair-competition exposure

## Channel plan (2-person team, mostly organic + a small launch voucher budget)

Since it's just two of you doing marketing + drinks + delivery admin, don't run 4 channels — pick 2-3 and go deep.

1. **Delivery platforms as the primary discovery channel** — GrabFood + ShopeeFood listing quality *is* the main acquisition lever at near-zero cost. Priorities:
   - Fully fill out the store listing (good photos, clear item names, accurate prep time)
   - Ask every satisfied customer to leave a platform review — reviews drive in-app ranking
   - **Apply for each platform's new-merchant promo program immediately** — ShopeeFood and GrabFood both run co-funded discount/free-ship programs for new stores; contact merchant support directly since terms change over time (Manus couldn't confirm current terms without a direct platform check)
2. **Zalo OA or Zalo group** — for direct orders outside platform commission (saves 20-25%/order) and repeat-customer broadcast. Optimal for reaching KDC Sông Đà and nearby residential clusters directly.
3. **Facebook local groups** — free, highest-ROI zero-budget channel for a 2-3km radius business.
4. Instagram — optional, lower priority given bandwidth. Only run it if one of you enjoys content.

## Concrete voucher/discount mechanics (ready to implement, within the 50% legal cap)
1. **First-order discount**: 15,000 VND off orders ≥50,000 VND. Weeks 1-2. (~25-30% of average order value, within the legal cap.)
2. **BOGO flash sale**: buy 1 get 1 free (same or lower-value item), limit 1/customer, run during a "golden hour" window (e.g. 2-4pm) or weekends. Weeks 1-2.
3. **Tiered discount by order value**: 10,000 VND off orders ≥80,000 VND; 20,000 VND off orders ≥120,000 VND. Continuous through month 1 — pushes average order value up.
4. **Free-shipping threshold**: free ship on orders ≥60,000 VND, stacked with platform co-funded free-ship programs where available. Continuous through month 1.

## Other tactics researched (medium priority, layer in as bandwidth allows)

- **Cross-promotion with nearby non-competing businesses**: gyms, salons, offices in the radius

## How to order
1. Customer finds SUNOI via GrabFood/ShopeeFood listing or a local FB post/Zalo broadcast →
2. Orders via the platform app, OR direct via Zalo (cheaper for repeat customers — no commission) →
3. If direct: confirm drink, size/sweetness/ice level, address, payment method →
4. Send confirmation message (see `02-operations/00-order-to-delivery-sop.md`) →
5. Delivery (platform-handled if platform order; team member/shipper if direct order) →
6. After delivery: ask for a platform review (platform orders) or a photo/testimonial (direct orders)

## Metrics to track weekly (see also `03-accounting-tracking/`)
- Orders/week, split by channel (platform vs. direct Zalo/FB)
- Repeat customer % (direct channel especially — this is what you're trying to grow, since it avoids commission)
- Most-ordered drink, platform rating/review count
- % of days hitting the 20-30 drink/day cap (signal to raise capacity or slow marketing push)
- Voucher redemption rate and cost-per-new-customer, against the 2-3M VND launch budget

## What a first-time VN F&B operator often misses (per Manus research)
- Basic food safety practices, even at small/informal scale
- Efficient inventory/fresh-ingredient management to avoid waste
- Personalized, warm customer care — Vietnamese residential customers weight this heavily
- Understanding VN drink preferences (sweetness levels, trà sữa/cà phê sữa đá conventions) in product decisions
- Delivery cost optimization within the 2-3km radius (route planning, encouraging free-ship-threshold orders)
- Building the owners' personal brand on Zalo/FB — builds local trust faster than a faceless business page
