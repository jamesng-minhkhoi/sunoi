## Before changing anything here
Marketing decisions at SUNOI are ranked by economics, not by appeal. Read `03-accounting-tracking/06-breakeven-storefront.md` first — a walk-in cup is worth ~2× a platform cup, and a second item in the basket is worth about as much as a whole extra order. Those two facts decide tactic priority.
