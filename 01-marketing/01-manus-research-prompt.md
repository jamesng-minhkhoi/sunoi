## Prompt

I'm launching **SUNOI**, a home-kitchen drinks delivery business (milk tea, coffee, fresh juice/smoothies) in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. This is a 1-2 month smoke test before we potentially expand into a chain of physical F&B locations, so I need marketing strategies that are cheap/scrappy now but could scale later — and I want this treated as reusable playbook material for future locations, not a one-off.

### Business context
- **Location**: Hiệp Bình ward, Thủ Đức, Ho Chi Minh City (near Phạm Văn Đồng street) — a residential area (KDC Sông Đà)
- **Delivery radius**: 2-3km from the home kitchen
- **Menu**: the public order page checked 2026-08-23 shows 20 entries across milk tea, tea, matcha, machine coffee, milk/coffee, and toppings. Listed base items are 12,000-45,000 VND and toppings are 12,000-15,000 VND; recipes, variants, availability, and average order value still need confirmation.
- **Team**: 2 people (a couple), no dedicated marketing hire, running this alongside kitchen ops and platform admin — assume roughly 4-8 combined marketing-hours/week available, not a full-time marketing function
- **Hours**: the public storefront displays 6:00-22:00; days of operation are not stated
- **Current ordering evidence**: the public SUNOI order page offers inner-city delivery or self-pickup. GrabFood, ShopeeFood, Baemin, direct Zalo, and local Facebook ordering should be treated as planned/unverified unless live platform/admin evidence is supplied.
- **Current marketing budget**: effectively 0 VND baseline (organic-only), but we're open to spending specifically on launch vouchers/discounts and grassroots promo if the ROI case is clear — give me a sense of what a "modest but real" launch voucher budget would be (e.g. is 500k VND meaningful, or do these campaigns typically need 2-5M+ VND to move the needle?)
- **Competitors**: I don't have reliable, current competitor data — please research this live as part of this task rather than me feeding you a list (any info I'd give you would be from a quick search that may already be stale — shops close, prices change, new entrants show up). Specifically find: drink shops (milk tea/coffee/juice) currently active and delivering within ~2-3km of Hiệp Bình ward, Thủ Đức, HCMC (near Phạm Văn Đồng street), ideally cross-checked against what's live on GrabFood/ShopeeFood right now — their price range, delivery/platform presence, any loyalty or discount mechanics they run, and approximate review volume/rating if visible. Flag your source and date for each competitor claim so I know how fresh it is — don't present anything as current fact without a checkable source.
- **Positioning**: brand name SUNOI (SUN + "oi", a warm Vietnamese term of endearment) — warm, personal, "someone you know" feel rather than corporate

### What I need researched

**1. Voucher / opening discount strategy, Vietnam-specific**
- What voucher/discount mechanics actually work for new F&B/drink brands in Vietnam (first-order codes, "mua 1 tặng 1" buy-one-get-one, flash sales, referral vouchers, loyalty stamp cards, tiered discounts by order value, etc.)?
- What's a realistic opening-week discount structure for a drinks brand whose public base items are currently 12k-45k VND plus 12k-15k toppings, with AOV and platform fees still unverified? Give concrete example numbers (e.g. "X% off first order, capped at Y VND, for the first Z orders") without assuming the old 30k-100k/order planning range is current.
- How do GrabFood and ShopeeFood's own built-in merchant promo tools work for brand-new stores (e.g. platform-subsidized discounts, boosted placement) — is this something we should apply for immediately, and what's the realistic approval/setup time?
- **Important**: Vietnam has legal limits on promotions (Nghị định 81/2018/NĐ-CP and related regulations cap max discount percentage and may require notifying local authorities for promotions above a certain scale/duration) — research what specifically applies to a small home-based, informally-operating seller at our scale, and what's realistically enforced vs. commonly ignored in practice. Be explicit about the actual legal risk, not just the letter of the law.

**2. School/nearby-institution outreach**
- Is promoting near schools/universities (flyering, sampling, partnering with student groups) a common and effective tactic for drink brands in Vietnam? What does it actually look like in practice (who do you talk to — school administration, student union, individual teachers; what age groups are appropriate to target given we're a drinks brand, not specifically targeting minors; any restrictions or etiquette norms around marketing near/at schools in Vietnam)?
- Are there schools, universities, offices, or other large daily-foot-traffic institutions within our 2-3km radius (Hiệp Bình ward, Thủ Đức, near Phạm Văn Đồng) worth researching as specific outreach targets? Name them if findable.
- What's the typical cost/effort/return for this kind of grassroots outreach compared to platform-based marketing — is it worth 2 people's time in week 1-4, or better suited for later once ops is proven?

**3. Other Vietnam-market-fit grassroots tactics**
Research and rank other low-cost, high-fit marketing tactics for a new home-based drinks brand in a Vietnamese urban residential area, such as:
- Sampling days / "mời uống thử" tasting booths — where, how, and what it typically costs in ingredients/time
- Check-in-for-discount social mechanics (chụp ảnh check-in nhận ưu đãi)
- Local mini/nano-influencer or KOL collaborations (what's a realistic budget and expected reach for a nano-influencer — roughly 1k-20k followers — in the HCMC F&B space specifically?)
- TikTok/livestream selling trends for F&B in Vietnam — is this worth 2 people's limited bandwidth at this stage?
- Group-buy ("mua chung") mechanics
- Zalo Mini App or Zalo OA broadcast tactics specific to Vietnamese consumer behavior
- Partnering with nearby businesses (gyms, offices, salons, other non-competing shops) for cross-promotion
- Any other tactics proven to work specifically for new home-kitchen or small independent F&B brands in Vietnam (not generic Western small-business marketing advice — I want what's proven to work in the VN market specifically)

### What I want back
1. A **prioritized list** of tactics (not generic advice) — each with: what it is, estimated cost in VND, expected effort (must fit a 2-person team with ~4-8 combined marketing-hours/week), expected impact/timeframe, and any legal/compliance flags specific to Vietnam
2. A recommended **sequencing/calendar** for the first 4 weeks post-launch, assuming we start with ~0 VND baseline budget but can allocate a modest, specific amount to a launch voucher campaign if justified — tell me what that amount should realistically be
3. Specific, concrete voucher/discount mechanics (not just "offer a discount") with example numbers appropriate to the live 12k-45k item-price baseline and likely basket sizes
4. Direct answer on whether school/institution outreach is worth doing in weeks 1-4 or should wait, with reasoning
5. Current, sourced competitor findings (names, prices, platform presence, any promo mechanics they run) with source/date for each claim
6. Anything Vietnam-specific I'm likely missing as a first-time F&B operator here

Where you're uncertain or a claim depends on fast-changing information (competitor pricing, platform promo programs, specific legal thresholds), say so explicitly rather than presenting it as settled fact — I'd rather know what needs a manual double-check than be given false confidence.
