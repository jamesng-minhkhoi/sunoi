## The core reframe (the most valuable part of the brief)

SUNOI's problem is not "marketing" in the abstract. It's the whole local demand system:

> **Traffic × reason to buy × price × convenience × repeatability**

A beautiful brand with good product still fails if not enough nearby people know it exists. Conversely a plain, small shop can do well on: high foot/road traffic, a simple menu, low friction, easy parking, fast prep, fair price, word of mouth, and repeated local exposure.

Channel roles, as proposed:

| Channel | Job |
|---|---|
| TikTok | Discovery / manufacture attention |
| Google Maps + reviews | Validation ("is this real and any good?") |
| Facebook / local groups | Neighbourhood distribution |
| Delivery apps | Convenience conversion inside the 2–3km catchment |
| The storefront itself | Street-level conversion, especially at night |
| Promotions | First purchase only |
| Product + speed + packaging | Turning a first purchase into a second |
| Referral | Cheapest acquisition available |
| Loyalty / new products | Repeat |

**Principle**: use social to manufacture *attention*; use product, price, convenience and referral to manufacture *habit*.

## Proposal A — Local positioning: own "Hiệp Bình", not "HCMC"

Rather than competing as a café in Ho Chi Minh City, become the obvious answer to a much smaller question: **đồ uống Hiệp Bình**.

Language to appear in content, Maps listing, and posts:
- Quán nước mới ở Hiệp Bình
- Trà đào Hiệp Bình / Cafe Hiệp Bình
- Ăn gì uống gì ở Hiệp Bình?
- Quán nước gần nhà ở Hiệp Bình
- 30k uống gì ở Hiệp Bình?
- Quán nước gần đây

Positioning line, consistent with the SUNOI brand (warm, personal, "someone you know" — not premium):

> **"Một quán nước nhỏ ở Hiệp Bình."** / **"Sunoi — nước uống gần nhà."**

This is the one proposal that costs nothing and conflicts with nothing. Reasonable to start immediately.

## Proposal B — TikTok as the discovery engine `[proposal, unvalidated]`

Proposed volume: ~5–10 low-production clips/day, not polished ads. Format ideas:

1. "Sunoi mới mở ở Hiệp Bình."
2. Making the signature drink
3. "Hôm nay bán được 17 ly."
4. Customer reaction
5. "Trà này 30k có đáng không?"
6. Drink + snack combo
7. Nighttime storefront
8. Packing a delivery order
9. New-product test: "có nên đưa vào menu không?"

**Honest caveat to keep attached to this idea:** the brief itself notes the well-known Vietnamese pattern where TikTok/KOC exposure creates a traffic spike that evaporates because the product/experience doesn't produce repeat customers. TikTok is a traffic generator, not a business. Volume of posting is also a real time cost for a 2-person team already running ~14 hour days — this competes directly with sleep.

## Proposal C — Many small local creators, not one big one `[proposal]`

Target profile: ~1k–20k followers, food/drink content, audience concentrated in **Thủ Đức / Hiệp Bình**. A creator with 3k local followers beats one with 300k spread across Vietnam.

Approach — do NOT commission an obvious advertisement. Proposed message:

> "Tụi mình mới mở ở Hiệp Bình. Ghé thử 2 món, nói thật cảm nhận. Nếu dở cứ nói dở."

Avoid large upfront fees. Start with free product + store credit.

## Proposal D — Referral / "Bạn của Sunoi" program `[proposal — ECONOMICS NOT VALIDATED]`

Concept: turn friends and happy customers into distributed marketers. Frame it socially ("Rủ bạn uống Sunoi"), not as a corporate affiliate scheme.

Proposed mechanic: each partner gets a code (e.g. `SUNOI-JAMES`). A genuinely new customer using it gets **10k off**; the referrer gets **10k store credit** (credit, not cash).

Proposed tiers — *illustrative only*:

| Referral | New customer | Partner |
|---|---|---|
| 1st–2nd | 10k off | 10k credit |
| 3rd | 10k off | 15k credit |
| 5th | 10k off | 25k bonus |
| 10th | 10k off | 50k bonus |

Proposed levels: **Friend** (anyone) → **Sunoi Partner** (≥5 successful referrals/month; better rate, early access to new drinks) → **Local Creator** (has an audience; credit or per-new-customer fee).

Who to recruit: students, apartment residents, office workers, gym members, nearby shop staff, barbers/nail salons, local TikTok creators, existing regulars.

**Anti-abuse rules (these are sound and worth keeping whatever the numbers become):**
- Reward only on a **completed qualifying purchase by a genuinely new customer** — never just for sharing a code
- One reward per new customer; minimum order value; no self-referral; no fake accounts
- Existing customers can't be re-counted as new
- Partner credit expires after 30–60 days

**Measure**: new customers acquired *per active partner* — never the number of partners signed up.

> ⚠️ **Do not launch these numbers as written.** Past SUNOI margin work (2026-08-29) used a ~15,000đ cost per cup with 30% platform commission — a 10k discount + 10k credit is roughly **a full cup of contribution given away per referral**, and on a platform order it is likely underwater. Recompute against real COGS (which itself is unresolved — see the roadmap open questions) before offering anything. The *structure* is good; the *amounts* are placeholders.

### Physical referral cards
Likely more effective locally than any digital system. Give each customer 3–5 cards to hand out:

> **Bạn của Sunoi**
> Tặng bạn **10K** cho lần đầu ghé Sunoi.
> Code: **JAMES**
> Người giới thiệu cũng nhận **10K Sunoi**.

## Proposal E — Menu: trendy + classic + signature `[proposal]`

- Keep a small core: a few reliable classics + one SUNOI signature
- Add **one or two trendy drinks** — Vietnamese social media can create demand for a specific drink very fast
- Introduce **one new/experimental item every 1–2 weeks** rather than constantly redesigning the menu — and use the development itself as content ("có nên đưa món này vào menu không?")

### Light food / snacks
Vietnamese customers often expect **drink + something light to eat**. Nearby competitors reportedly sell bánh tráng, cá viên, phô mai que, khoai tây chiên, flan.

Recommendation: **do not** become a food shop. Test **2–3 easy-to-operate items** to capture "cho em thêm cái này" — raising average order value and giving groups a reason to stay. Note this trades against prep time and adds inventory/waste risk in a 2-person operation.

## Proposal F — The storefront as a conversion surface `[proposal]`

In ~2 seconds from the street at night, a passer-by should register:

> SUNOI → DRINKS → LOOKS GOOD → WANT TO TRY

Checklist: stronger night lighting; visible drinks; a visually striking signature drink; clear legible signage; clean frontage; obvious/easy parking and stopping; visible QR for ordering; visible snacks. The storefront doubles as TikTok content.

---

## Proposal G — Reduce discount dependence by week 3 `[proposal]`

Avoid training customers to buy only on promotion. Proposed ladder instead of permanent discounting:

- 1st purchase → opening offer
- 2nd purchase → free topping
- 3rd purchase → free size upgrade

Keep it analog: *"Lần sau mang bill cũ → topping free."* No loyalty app.

---

## Proposal H — Find out who the customer actually is (week 4)

Tag every order by type — student / nearby resident / office worker / family / delivery / passer-by — and answer: who buys, who comes back, who spends most, who orders food, who came from TikTok, who only buys on promotion. **This determines what SUNOI actually is.** It depends entirely on the order log being filled in.

## Explicit "don't do" list (from the brief — reasonable as written)

- Don't pour money into Facebook ads immediately
- Don't pay large influencers
- Don't build a loyalty app
- Don't build a huge menu
- Don't run constant discounts
- Don't use generic corporate brand messaging
- Don't target all of HCMC
- Don't assume Google Maps alone generates demand
- Don't assume a beautiful interior brings customers
- Don't assume TikTok views equal revenue
- Don't run 14 hours just because cafés traditionally do
- Don't copy North American F&B marketing unadapted

## Decision rule for every experiment above
**KEEP** (works) / **CHANGE** (promising, needs adjustment) / **KILL** (no evidence after a fair test). Decide on a date and a number, not on a feeling.

## What this brief got wrong or can't support

Recorded so the next reader doesn't treat it as gospel:

1. **The hour-by-hour order table in the source brief was a hypothetical example** (it totalled 31 orders/day, contradicting the actual ~5/day). It is deliberately **not** reproduced here as data. The real action is to start recording the hour of each order — see `03-accounting-tracking/01-order-log.csv`.
2. **The revenue ladder (30 orders × 50k = 1.5M/day, etc.) assumes a 50k AOV that has never been measured** at SUNOI. Note that the 2026-08-29 ShopeeFood work deliberately used a 50,000đ *minimum order gate* — a floor, not an average. Don't conflate the two.
3. **Referral amounts are unvalidated against margin** — see the warning in Proposal D.
4. **The competitor list** in the source brief is unverified and differs from the list already on file; both are recorded in `../history/2026-07-25-launch-plan-home-kitchen.md` with dates and caveats.
5. **Time cost is unaccounted for.** Roughly: 5–10 TikToks/day + creator outreach + referral admin + snack prep + new product every 2 weeks. Two people running a ~14 hour shop cannot do all of this. **Sequencing is the missing piece of this brief** — pick one or two things, not fifteen.

## Suggested sequencing (added here, not from the source brief)

**This section is now superseded by `../00-marketing-plan.md` §7, which carries the same sequence in decided form.** Kept here for the reasoning.

**SUNOI relaunches 2026-09-14. Nothing in this file should be attempted before then** — Two people cannot execute fifteen ideas in two days and also open a shop.

0. **Before Day 0 (2026-09-14)**: platform + Google Maps address live; opening offer decided (blocked on real COGS); stock; order log ready; storefront legible at night; Zalo broadcast + local FB post. Proposal A (local positioning language) is free and can start immediately.
1. **Days 1–7 after Day 0**: log every order with hour + channel. Ask every walk-in for a Google Maps review. Nothing else.
2. **Week 2**: chart orders by hour, set real opening hours. Then pick **ONE** acquisition channel — local TikTok (Proposal B) is the strongest candidate — and run it hard for two weeks with a KEEP/CHANGE/KILL decision at the end.
3. **Week 4+**: referral program (Proposal D), with numbers recomputed against real COGS and against the ~267,000đ/day fixed-cost line.
4. **Later**: snacks, trendy-drink cycle, partner tiers.

**A note the source brief couldn't have made**: fixed costs are **~267,000đ/day** (rent 6M/month rising to 7M, plus ~2M utilities). Two consequences that should change how you read every proposal above:
- **A walk-in or direct cup is worth roughly twice a platform cup.** Break-even is ~11–13 walk-in/direct orders a day versus 22–28 platform ones. Proposals A, F and D — which drive people to the physical shop or to direct ordering — are worth materially more than anything that drives platform volume.
- **A second item in the basket is worth about as much as a whole extra order.** Two cups per order roughly halves the break-even count (to ~5/day walk-in). That makes **Proposal E's snack test the highest-leverage item on this list after launch** — not because snacks are a good business on their own, but because "cho em thêm cái này" is a cheaper lever than finding twice as many customers.
