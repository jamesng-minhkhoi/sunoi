# SUNOI — Marketing Plan

**The single current marketing plan. Start here.** Written 2026-09-12, two days before the relaunch. Supersedes the scattered docs listed at the bottom.

| | |

Marketing's job for the next 30 days is one thing: **get from ~5 orders/day to 11–13 walk-in/direct orders a day.** Not brand building, not a revenue target, not 10M/day.

## 2. The economic frame — this decides everything below

Two facts from `03-accounting-tracking/06-breakeven-storefront.md` should drive every channel choice, ahead of how appealing any given tactic sounds:

> **A walk-in or direct cup is worth about twice a platform cup.**
> 39k list, 15k COGS → 24,000đ walk-in vs 12,300đ on a platform after 30% commission.

> **A second item in the basket is worth about as much as a whole extra order.**
> Two walk-in cups (39k + 45k) contribute 54,000đ, so ~5 orders/day breaks even instead of ~11.

**Therefore, channels and tactics rank in this order:**

| Priority | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Drives **walk-in / direct** orders | Worth 2× a platform order |
| **2** | Raises **basket size** (2nd item, upsell, snacks) | Halves the order count needed — cheaper than finding twice as many customers |
| **3** | Raises **price point** | 35k → 45k nearly doubles platform contribution |
| **4** | Drives **platform volume** | Real, but the least valuable order SUNOI can take |

Delivery apps stay switched on — they're convenience and discovery. They just don't get the effort budget that the storefront and direct channel get.

## 3. Positioning

Warm, personal, local, "someone you know". **Not premium, not corporate.** The brand name carries it: SUN + *ơi*.

Own a small geography rather than competing city-wide:

> **"Một quán nước nhỏ ở Hiệp Bình."** — or — **"Sunoi — nước uống gần nhà."**

Use this language everywhere (Maps listing, captions, posts, replies) so SUNOI + HIỆP BÌNH appear together repeatedly:

- Quán nước mới ở Hiệp Bình · Trà đào Hiệp Bình · Cafe Hiệp Bình
- Ăn gì uống gì ở Hiệp Bình? · Quán nước gần nhà ở Hiệp Bình
- 30k uống gì ở Hiệp Bình? · Quán nước gần đây

Local customers ask *"Có gì ngon? Bao nhiêu tiền? Có deal không? Có topping không? Có ship không? Gần nhà không? Có đáng thử không?"* — answer those, in that language. Not brand values.

**This costs nothing and starts immediately.**

## 4. Channel roles

| Channel | Job | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| **Storefront** | Street-level conversion, especially after dark. Lighting, signage, visible drinks, easy stopping/parking. Also free content. | **1** |
| **Google Maps** | Validation. Someone hears about SUNOI, then checks if it looks real. Review count is the barrier — competitors sit at 380–436, SUNOI at ~zero. | **1** |
| **Zalo + local FB groups** | Neighbourhood distribution, and the direct channel that avoids 30% commission | **1** |
| **TikTok** | Discovery — manufacture attention. Local, low-production, high-volume. | 2 (from week 2) |
| **Football nights** | Event-driven walk-in traffic with long dwell and big baskets — hits priorities 1 and 2 at once (§9) | **1** (on match days) |
| **Partner program** | Cheapest acquisition available, and it produces walk-ins, not platform orders (§8) | 2 (from week 4) |
| **Delivery apps** | Convenience conversion. Keep listings good; don't pour effort here. | 3 |

**The funnel**: TikTok/storefront/word-of-mouth → "what is this?" → Google Maps + reviews → "looks real" → first order (promo) → good product, fast, clean packaging → second order → referral → local habit.

**Attention vs. habit**: social media manufactures attention; product, price, convenience and referral manufacture habit. Vietnamese F&B is full of shops that got a TikTok spike and died anyway because nothing brought people back. Treat views as a leading indicator, never as revenue.

## 6. Opening offer

**Objective is sampling velocity, not margin.** Get as many local people as possible to taste the product once.

**Proposed**: *Mua 1 tặng 1* on **one or two designated drinks** for the first 2–3 days. Not the whole menu.

**Make it in-store only.** Three reasons: it drives physical discovery, it protects platform margin, and a walk-in cup is worth twice a platform cup anyway.

> ⚠️ **Still blocked on COGS.** At ~15,000đ/cup a BOGO gives away a full cup of contribution; on a platform order at a 35k list price it's underwater. At ~9,000đ it's comfortable. **Both figures are on record from the same August session** — confirm the real one before committing. This is a 2-day deadline.

### Legal — what actually applies (carried forward, still current)
Per Nghị định 81/2018/NĐ-CP and its 2026 amendment (Nghị định 239/2026/NĐ-CP):
- **Max discount: 50%** of pre-promotion price. BOGO on a same-or-lower-value item sits at the cap — fine, don't exceed it.
- **Sở Công Thương notification** (normally ≥3 working days ahead) is **exempted** when total prize/gift value is under 100 million VND **or** the promotion runs only through an e-commerce platform. **Both exemptions apply to SUNOI**, so no advance notification is needed at this scale.
- **Prohibited**: running promotions physically at schools, hospitals or government offices. Nearby schools are a real audience — reach them through online channels, never at the gate.

### Other mechanics worth using later (within the 50% cap)
- First-order discount: 15,000đ off orders ≥50,000đ
- Tiered by order value: 10,000đ off ≥80,000đ; 20,000đ off ≥120,000đ — **this one directly attacks basket size, priority 2**
- Free-ship threshold ≥60,000đ, stacked with platform co-funded programs
- **Apply to each platform's new-merchant promo program** — ShopeeFood and GrabFood co-fund discounts for new stores; terms shift, so ask merchant support directly

## 7. The 30-day sequence

**One thing at a time.** Two people cannot run five channels and a shop. Each phase ends with a **KEEP / CHANGE / KILL** call on a date and a number, not a feeling.

### Week 1 (Day 0 → Day 7) — trial and proof
- Run the opening offer, in-store
- **Log every order: hour, channel, new/repeat.** Launch week is the only week with a spike; it's the baseline everything later is measured against and it can't be reconstructed
- **Ask every walk-in customer for a Google Maps review, in person.** Day 0 customers are the friendliest audience SUNOI will ever have. Closing the review gap is the single cheapest credibility gain available
- Nothing else. No TikTok system, no KOC outreach, no referral program.

### Week 2 — one acquisition channel, hard
- Chart orders by hour → **set real opening hours from the data**, don't keep running ~14h out of habit
- Pick **ONE** channel and run it properly for two weeks. **Local TikTok is the strongest candidate**: low-production, local, high-volume. Formats that work — "Sunoi mới mở ở Hiệp Bình", making the signature drink, "hôm nay bán được 17 ly", customer reactions, "trà này 30k có đáng không?", the storefront at night, packing an order
- Start contacting **small local creators** (~1k–20k followers, audience concentrated in Thủ Đức/Hiệp Bình). A creator with 3k local followers beats one with 300k spread across Vietnam. Offer free product + store credit, never a large upfront fee
- Don't commission an advertisement. Ask for honesty: *"Tụi mình mới mở ở Hiệp Bình. Ghé thử 2 món, nói thật cảm nhận. Nếu dở cứ nói dở."*

### Week 3 — basket size (priority 2, and the cheapest lever available)
- **Start football nights** if the screen and connection are sorted — the match-night combo and the snack test are the same experiment, run them together (§9)
- Test **2–3 easy light-food items** — bánh tráng, phô mai que, khoai tây chiên, flan are common nearby. **Not** a food menu; just enough to capture *"cho em thêm cái này"*
- Introduce the tiered by-order-value discount to pull baskets up
- Start moving off blanket discounting toward repeat incentives: 2nd purchase → free topping, 3rd → free size upgrade. Keep it analog — *"lần sau mang bill cũ → topping free."* No loyalty app
- Introduce one new/experimental drink and make the development itself content: *"có nên đưa món này vào menu không?"*

### Week 4 — partner program, and find out who the customer is
- Launch **"Bạn của Sunoi"** — milestone cashback, walk-in/direct only. Full mechanic, ladder and rules in **§8**
- Recruit existing regulars first; they're the easiest ask
- Tag every order by customer type (student / resident / office / family / delivery / passer-by) and answer: who buys, who comes back, who spends most, who orders food, who came from TikTok, who only buys on promotion. **This determines what SUNOI actually is.**

## 8. Partner program — "Bạn của Sunoi"

**Decided mechanic: milestone cashback.** A partner earns a payout each time their code reaches a milestone of qualifying orders — not a trickle per referral.

Milestone-based is the right call for a shop this size. It means far fewer payouts to administer, it self-selects (only people genuinely bringing customers ever reach 10), and it doesn't pay out for a single fluke referral. It also gives the partner a visible target to chase.

### The ladder

| Milestone | Payout | Per order | Cost as % of contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| **10 qualifying orders** | **50,000đ** | 5,000đ | ~14% (at 1.5 cups/order) |
| **20 qualifying orders** | **120,000đ** | 6,000đ | ~17% |
| Every 20 after that | 120,000đ | 6,000đ | ~17% |

The second milestone pays slightly better per order on purpose — it rewards continuing rather than stopping at 10.

**These are affordable.** At 39k list / 15k COGS, a walk-in order at 1.5 cups generates ~36,000đ of contribution; paying 5–6,000đ of that for a customer SUNOI didn't have is cheap acquisition — far cheaper than the 30% a delivery platform takes on every single order forever.

> ⚠️ **Recompute if COGS turns out to be 15k and baskets are single-cup.** At 1 cup/order the 10-order tier costs ~21% of contribution — still fine, but the margin for error shrinks. The whole ladder assumes the real COGS number; confirm it.

### Rule that falls straight out of the economics
> **Partner codes work on walk-in and direct orders only. Not on platform orders.**

A platform cup contributes 12,300đ against a walk-in cup's 24,000đ. Paying 5–6,000đ/order cashback on top of a 30% platform commission eats roughly half the contribution. Partner-driven orders should come *to the shop* or *through Zalo* — which is also exactly where SUNOI wants them.

### Cash or credit
James's instinct is cash, and that's right for motivation — many good partners (students, staff at nearby shops, gym members) aren't daily drinkers, so store credit is worth little to them.

**Offer both, priced to steer**: at the 20-order milestone, **120,000đ cash or 180,000đ in SUNOI credit.** Credit costs SUNOI COGS rather than cash and brings the partner back into the shop, so it can be worth ~50% more to them and still cost less. Let them choose.

### What counts as a qualifying order
- Uses the partner's code, **at the counter or via Zalo** (not platform)
- **Minimum order value** — set it at the same level as the basket target, so referrals pull baskets up rather than down
- **Max 1 per customer per day** — stops one friend ordering ten times to farm a milestone
- Paid and completed. Cancelled orders don't count.
- **No self-referral.** A partner's own orders never count toward their own tally.

### Tracking — keep it on paper
No app. A single sheet per partner: code, date, order value. The order log's `Acquisition Source` column captures the code, so the tally can be reconstructed from `03-accounting-tracking/01-order-log.csv` if a partner disputes it. Pay out when the milestone is hit, in person — that conversation is itself a retention moment.

### Who to recruit
Students, apartment residents, office workers, gym members, staff at nearby non-competing shops, barbers, nail salons, local creators, and **existing regulars first** — someone who already likes SUNOI is the easiest ask.

**Measure new customers per active partner, never the number of partners signed up.** A list of 30 partners where 25 do nothing is a worse result than 5 who each bring 20 orders.

### Physical cards
More effective locally than any digital system. Give each customer 3–5 to hand out:

> **Bạn của Sunoi**
> Tặng bạn ưu đãi cho lần đầu ghé Sunoi.
> Code: **[TÊN]**
> Người giới thiệu tích đủ 10 đơn → nhận **50K**.

## 9. Football streaming nights

**Event-driven traffic on match days.** This is the strongest single idea on this page for SUNOI's specific economics, for four reasons:

1. **It drives walk-in** — the order type worth 2× a platform order (§2, priority 1)
2. **It raises basket size** — people sit for 90+ minutes and order more than once (§2, priority 2). This is the lever that halves break-even
3. **It uses the storefront at night**, when the lighting and street presence are already doing work
4. **It's a reason to come that isn't "I want a drink"** — which is the thing SUNOI currently lacks, and it fits "một quán nước nhỏ ở Hiệp Bình" perfectly

### What one night is worth

Walk-in contribution at 39k list / 15k COGS. Daily fixed cost is ~267,000đ.

| People | Items each | Cups | Contribution | = days of fixed cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 2 | 16 | 384,000đ | 1.4 |
| 15 | 2 | 30 | **720,000đ** | **2.7** |
| 15 | 3 | 45 | 1,080,000đ | 4.0 |
| 25 | 2 | 50 | 1,200,000đ | 4.5 |

**A single decent match night pays for two to four ordinary days.** That changes what "a slow Tuesday" costs.

### Which matches
Build the calendar from what people in Hiệp Bình actually care about, roughly in order of draw:
1. **Vietnam national team** — by far the biggest, and worth treating as a special event rather than a normal night
2. **Premier League** — large, reliable Vietnamese following; late-evening kickoffs suit a drinks shop
3. **Champions League** — late nights, smaller but committed crowd
4. **V-League** and local derbies

Check the fixture list at the start of each week and mark the nights. **Don't promise "football every night"** — scarcity is part of why people turn up, and it protects the hours.

### Make the night pay
- **A combo, not a single drink.** Someone nursing one 39k drink for two hours is a loss of a seat. Offer a match-night combo — drink + snack, or two drinks at a small saving. This is the same basket logic as §2, and it's the natural pairing for the light-food test in week 3.
- **Announce it.** A post the day before in local FB groups and on Zalo, and a sign outside on the day: *"Tối nay có bóng đá — [trận đấu], [giờ]."*
- **Film it.** A full shop watching a match is the best TikTok content SUNOI will ever get for free, and it proves the place is alive. Good for the Maps listing photos too.
- **Ask for reviews on the way out.** A crowd in a good mood after a win is the single best moment to close the review gap.

### Before running one — check these
- [ ] **Screen and connection**: TV or projector, and internet stable enough for live video. A stream that buffers through a goal is worse than not running the night.
- [ ] ⚠️ **Broadcast rights.** Screening football publicly in a commercial venue in Vietnam is licensed — rights sit with holders such as K+ / VTVcab, and a commercial screening licence is technically required. Plenty of small quán screen informally, and enforcement against a shop this size is uncommon, but it is a real exposure and it compounds with SUNOI's unregistered status. **Know that you're making that choice rather than discovering it later.** A legitimate subscription package is the clean version if the numbers above justify it — one or two good nights cover a month of most packages.
- [ ] **Seating and capacity** — how many people can actually sit and watch? That number caps everything in the table above.
- [ ] **Hours and staffing.** Late kickoffs mean late closes for two people already working long days. **Match nights are a strong argument for shifting hours later rather than extending them** — a late match night should replace a dead morning, not stack on top of it. The hour-by-hour order data from week 1 will show which mornings are dead.
- [ ] **Noise and neighbours** — a crowd reacting to a goal at 2am in a residential area is a complaint risk worth thinking about once.

## 10. Budget

**2–3 million VND was allocated for a 4-week voucher campaign** (~750k/week). That figure was set when SUNOI had **zero fixed costs**. It now sits on top of rent:

> ~750k/week ≈ **107,000đ/day of promo spend**, on top of ~267,000đ/day fixed = **~374,000đ/day during the launch window**.
> At 39k/15k that's **~15.6 walk-in cups/day to break even during the promo, versus ~11.1 without it.**

That's not a reason to skip it — trial has to be bought somehow, and 5 orders/day is nowhere. It is a reason to be strict about **what the spend has to prove**:

> **Decision rule**: the promo is working if **week-2 repeat customers appear**. Trial volume alone is not success — it's the thing everyone mistakes for success right before the spike evaporates.

Everything else stays organic. No Facebook ad spend. No paid influencers.

## 12. Metrics — weekly

Straight out of `03-accounting-tracking/01-order-log.csv`:

- **Orders split three ways: walk-in / platform / direct** — the walk-in share is the whole point of having a shop
- **Orders by hour** — sets the opening hours
- **New vs. repeat** — at ~5 orders/day the question isn't margin, it's whether anyone comes back
- **AOV and cups per order** — never yet measured, and per §2 the cups-per-order figure may be the cheapest lever available
- **Daily contribution vs. ~267,000đ** — the one number to check at close of business
- Google Maps review count · most-ordered item · TikTok posts and views · creators contacted · voucher redemptions and cost per new customer
- **Partner program**: qualifying orders per *active* partner, milestones paid, cost per acquired order — never the headline partner count (§8)
- **Football nights**: people in, cups sold, contribution vs. the ~267,000đ line, and whether any of them came back on a non-match day (§9). That last one is the whole question — a match-night crowd that never returns is an expensive audience, not a customer base.
