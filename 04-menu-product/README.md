# 04-menu-product

**Answers**: what does SUNOI actually sell?

## Status: 🔴 the longest-standing blocker in the repo
The full drink list has never been put here — open since 2026-07-25. Without it: no per-item pricing, no margin check, no recipe specs for the ops SOP, no platform listing copy, and no honest promo or referral economics.

**Drop the menu here** (doc, PDF, or photos) and it can be turned into all of the above.

## Open product questions
- [ ] **Full item list with names, sizes and list prices** — the blocker
- [ ] **Real cost per cup.** Two conflicting figures are on record from 2026-08-29: ~9,000đ ("to be safe") and ~15,000đ (used in the margin model). Both can't be right, and every discount decision depends on which it is.
- [ ] Does Coffee CheeseCream have size variants?
- [ ] **A signature drink** — is there one, and is it visually striking enough to be worth photographing/filming? (see Proposal E/F in `01-marketing/history/2026-09-12-storefront-strategy-brief.md`)
- [ ] **Light food / snacks** — proposed as a 2–3 item test (bánh tráng, phô mai que, khoai tây chiên, flan and similar are common at nearby competitors) to capture "cho em thêm cái này" and raise average order value. Not decided. Trades against prep time and adds waste risk for a 2-person team.
- [ ] **Trendy drink slot** — proposal is to carry one or two trend-driven drinks alongside the classics, and introduce one new/experimental item every 1–2 weeks rather than redesigning the whole menu.
- [ ] Drink photography for platform listings and content — flagged missing since July, still missing, and it directly affects delivery-app conversion.
