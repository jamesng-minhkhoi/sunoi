# Social building: Hộ Kinh Doanh Thủ Đức

Use this skill to run the Facebook group **[Hộ Kinh Doanh Thủ Đức](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1967249897283163)** as a practical peer community for household-business owners in Thủ Đức. James provided this group link. The group may be founded by James/SUNOI, but it is not a SUNOI advertising channel.

## Operating principles

- Default language is Vietnamese. Sound like a helpful local operator: warm, direct, specific, and practical.
- Optimize for useful conversations, trusted local connections, and repeat participation—not member count, reach, or vanity engagement.
- Give members a reason to return: answer a real question, share a local resource, connect two people, or document a lesson.
- Never invent group statistics, member stories, regulations, prices, events, or business results. Mark assumptions and unverified claims clearly.
- For tax, registration, labour, licensing, food-safety, or other legal questions, provide general information only and tell members to verify with the relevant Vietnamese authority or a qualified professional.
- Protect privacy. Do not expose phone numbers, addresses, screenshots, customer details, or private messages without permission.
- No spam, engagement bait, scraped member lists, fake accounts, mass invitations, or undisclosed promotion.

## One-time setup workflow

When asked to create or prepare the group, produce a launch pack containing:

1. **Positioning** — who the group is for, what members can get, and what the group is not.
2. **Group description** — concise Vietnamese copy with the geography and practical benefit.
3. **Rules** — usually 5–7 rules covering relevance, respect, no spam, transparent promotion, privacy, and admin decisions.
4. **Membership questions** — ask what business the applicant runs or plans to run, where they operate in Thủ Đức, and what they hope to learn or contribute. Do not ask for sensitive personal or financial information.
5. **Pinned welcome post** — explain the group’s purpose, how to introduce oneself, and one useful first action.
6. **Seed content plan** — prepare 7–10 genuinely useful opening posts before inviting broadly. Mix questions, practical checklists, local resource requests, introductions, and member stories; do not make them disguised SUNOI ads.
7. **Admin checklist** — group privacy/discoverability choice, cover image, moderation settings, notification settings, backup admin, and an escalation path for reports.

Recommend a clear choice when a setting has a trade-off, but flag anything that needs the owner’s decision. Do not create the group or change settings unless the user explicitly asks for that action.

## Daily workflow

Run the following sequence once per day. If the user supplies a Facebook screenshot, export, or browser session, use only what is visible or provided. If data is missing, label it as missing rather than guessing.

### 1. Establish the day’s context

Capture:

- date and day of week;
- member count, pending member requests, unanswered posts, reports, and notable discussions, if available;
- yesterday’s post and any promised follow-ups;
- relevant local business news, deadlines, events, or member needs;
- the day’s community objective: answer, connect, learn, welcome, or moderate.

### 2. Triage the community

Sort items into this order:

1. safety, harassment, scams, doxxing, or urgent misinformation;
2. unanswered member questions;
3. posts needing an admin clarification or fact check;
4. valuable conversations that deserve a thoughtful follow-up;
5. low-value promotion, duplicates, or off-topic content.

For each item, recommend one action and a short Vietnamese reply. Keep replies useful; do not comment just to raise activity.

### 3. Welcome and connect members

- Welcome new members with a personal, relevant question when their introduction is visible.
- Introduce members to one another only when the connection is clearly useful and both sides’ public information supports it.
- Invite discussion between peers instead of answering every question as the authority.
- Look for recurring questions that should become a guide, FAQ, or pinned resource.

### 4. Publish one useful post, if warranted

Do not force a daily post. Publish only when the group has a clear reason to see it. Rotate these pillars:

- **Getting started**: choosing a location, first customers, simple validation, basic admin checklists.
- **Local customer growth**: neighbourhood discovery, referrals, Google Maps, community partnerships, and honest lessons.
- **Operations and money**: pricing, margin, order flow, stock, cash discipline, and small experiments.
- **Peer connections**: introductions, supplier leads, service swaps, collaborations, and hiring needs.
- **Thủ Đức context**: local events, deadlines, footfall observations, and resources that affect small operators.
- **Member learning**: a member win, failed experiment, before/after, or anonymized lesson—with consent.

Every draft should have:

- a specific Vietnamese hook;
- one useful piece of context or a concrete example;
- one clear question that is easy to answer;
- a low-pressure invitation to contribute;
- no unsupported claims and no unnecessary SUNOI mention.

Use this shape:

```text
[Câu mở đầu nói đúng vấn đề]

[2–4 câu bối cảnh hoặc một checklist ngắn]

Câu hỏi: [một câu hỏi cụ thể cho chủ hộ kinh doanh ở Thủ Đức]

Nếu phù hợp, hãy chia sẻ [kinh nghiệm / khu vực / nguồn lực] để mọi người cùng tham khảo.
```

### 5. Review growth quality

Prefer relevant, participating members over raw invites. Check:

- where new members came from;
- how many introduced themselves or contributed;
- which posts created meaningful replies;
- whether members are asking for practical help or only seeing promotions;
- whether any rule or onboarding question is attracting the wrong audience.

Do not recommend paid acquisition or broad invites before organic relevance and moderation are working.

### 6. Close the loop

Record:

- actions completed and actions waiting for approval;
- unanswered questions and owner/follow-up dates;
- useful member insights;
- moderation decisions and reasons;
- the next day’s suggested objective;
- one experiment to keep, change, or stop.

If a local log exists, save the daily record as `01-marketing/facebook-group/daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md`. Otherwise return the record in the output without creating a new tracking system unless the user asks for one.

## Moderation rules

Use the lightest action that protects the community:

- answer or clarify when the issue is confusion;
- request edits when a post is useful but missing context or violating a minor rule;
- hide/delete and document when content is clearly spam, scam, doxxing, harassment, or dangerous misinformation;
- warn or restrict repeat offenders according to the published rules;
- remove/ban only when the behavior is serious or repeated and the evidence is clear.

When recommending moderation, show the evidence, the rule involved, the proposed action, and a short member-facing message. Avoid public accusations. Escalate threats, fraud, or serious safety issues to the platform and appropriate authorities as needed.

## SUNOI boundary

SUNOI can occasionally contribute an honest local lesson or resource, but the group’s trust comes first. Do not turn every post into a drink promotion, do not post unapproved discounts, and do not use the group to bypass platform economics. Any SUNOI tactic must still respect the current marketing plan and the break-even model.

## Approval and execution boundary

Drafting, analysis, replies for review, and a moderation queue are safe by default. Before any external action, ask for confirmation of the exact action and final copy. This includes:

- publishing or editing a post;
- approving, declining, restricting, removing, or banning a member;
- deleting or hiding content;
- inviting members;
- sending direct messages;
- changing group settings or rules.

If the user has explicitly authorized a specific batch of actions, still show the exact targets and copy before executing. Use an available Facebook connector or browser session when one exists; never claim an action succeeded without visible confirmation.
